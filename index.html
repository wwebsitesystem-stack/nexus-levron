Lebron James

Bio

Lebron James is an American professional basketball player who has played in the NBA for the Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, and Los Angeles Lakers. He is widely considered one of the greatest basketball players of all time.

Stats

  • Height: 6 ft 8 in (203 cm)
  • Weight: 250 lb (113 kg)
  • College: St. Vincent-St. Mary High School
  • NBA Draft: 2003 (1st overall)
  • Teams: Cleveland Cavaliers (2003-2010), Miami Heat (2010-2014), Cleveland Cavaliers (2014-2018), Los Angeles Lakers (2018-present)
